SHARED SPI BUS:  SCK = GPIO18  ·  MOSI = GPIO23  ·  MISO = GPIO19   —   All three devices (nRF24 / TFT / Touch) share these lines. Chip Select (CS) isolates them.
⚠ CRITICAL — nRF24L01 Power: Always place a 100µF electrolytic capacitor directly across VCC and GND on the nRF24 module, as close to the module as possible. Omitting it causes missed detections, false positives, and random ESP32 resets.
⚠ POWER RAIL: The nRF24L01 must be powered from the ESP32 3.3V LDO rail — never directly from TP4056 OUT+ (which reaches 4.2V at full charge and will damage the module).
ℹ GPIO4 — DUAL ROLE (v3.1): GPIO4 serves as the XPT2046 T_IRQ touch interrupt during normal operation AND as the ext0 deep sleep wakeup source. A tap on the screen wakes the device without a power cycle.
